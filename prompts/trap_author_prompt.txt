TRAP FAMILIES (use the one named in FOCUS; invent a NEW instance, never reuse a published puzzle, riddle, or benchmark item):

2. Constraint implied by the goal: the purpose of the task implies a constraint that the obvious computation ignores, like the car needing to be at the car wash. Examples of the shape: a van must end its route at the depot that holds tomorrow's stock; a retention policy must never delete the only copy of a month; a meeting must fit the organizer's own availability. State the purpose plainly; once noticed, the constraint is unambiguous.
3. Stated non-default convention: dates written day-first, decimal commas, half-open ranges, 1-based numbering, weeks starting on Monday, a stated non-UTC zone, a non-alphabetical sort order. State it once in a normal sentence; the default makes the answer wrong.
4. Fencepost and counting language: "every 3rd day starting today", "a post every 2 m along a 10 m fence including both ends", "between 5 and 9 exclusive". The natural off-by-one reading is wrong.
5. Stated rule that differs from the language default: the requirement is ordinary business logic, but Python's default does something else, so the reflexive one-liner is wrong: halves round up (Python's round() rounds half to even), negative quantities truncate toward zero (// floors), remainders take the sign of the dividend (% takes the divisor's), consecutive delimiters produce empty fields (str.split() collapses whitespace), "file9" sorts before "file10" (sorted() is lexicographic), amounts are compared in exact cents (floats drift). Inputs where the default and the rule agree must not dominate the hidden tests.
7. Silent data-visible edge case: the instruction states only a general fact ("a payment is identified by its payment_id", "the ids were copy-pasted from emails and may contain invisible characters", "the export has the same format as /app/data/sample.csv", "rows are individual line items"), and a sample data file in /app/data (a reference file at data/<name>) contains the rare cases that make the natural implementation SILENTLY wrong: re-sent duplicates of the same id, zero-width or look-alike characters in identifiers, a unit or scale stated only in the header (for example "amount (k€)"), TOTAL or subtotal rows mixed into the data, continuation lines, or a column whose meaning changes after a marker row. The natural implementation must run without errors on the sample and produce plausible numbers; only actually inspecting the data reveals the issue, and once seen, the correct handling follows from the stated fact. Hidden tests generate files containing the same patterns. MEASURED: when the edge case was not determinable from anything the agent could see, 2 of 5 runs failed, but that is unfair; this family makes the edge case visible in the data instead.
8. Checklist breadth: ONE easy-looking task (a formatter, exporter, or summariser over small inputs) with 8-12 independent small requirements, each stated once in plain words and each one of the measured slip types: rounding mode, inclusive/exclusive bound, day-first dates, natural or stated sort order with a tie-break, case/whitespace normalisation, thousands separators, empty-field handling, singular/plural wording, trailing newline, a stated non-default default value. MEASURED: each single stated detail is missed in about 3-7% of runs (only in rushed runs), so about ten independent details are needed to land near a 50% pass rate; keep every requirement trivial so the task stays short.
6. Perspective and self-inclusion counting: a count seen from one member's point of view must include or exclude that member or a related one (like "Alice has 3 brothers and 2 sisters; how many sisters does her brother have?" where Alice herself counts).
